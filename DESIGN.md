## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the gravitas, violence, and solemn lore of two colliding mythic pantheons: Classical Greece and Old Norse Midgard. It presents the digital interface not as an ephemeral website, but as a living, weather-beaten imperial codex—bound in iron-reinforced calfskin, inscribed upon aged vellum, and illuminated by Spartan blood-oaths and Fimbulwinter frost.

### Aesthetic Movement
**Tactile & Skeuomorphic Historicism merged with Editorial Liturgical Layout.**
- **Greek Motif:** Burnished bronze fillets, meander fretwork friezes, and authoritative epigraphic serif lettering.
- **Norse Motif:** Deep cold iron hardware, crystalline runic incisions, knotwork borders, and frosted marginal flourishes.
- **Artifact Quality:** Heavy, textured, aged parchment planes, chiseled corner brackets, illuminated drop capitals, and marginalia annotations replicating hand-scribed codices.

### Emotional Target
The user feels like an archivist, war scholar, or god traversing forbidden realms. Every click carries the deliberate physical weight of flipping vellum or unlatching heavy bronze clasps.

## Colors

The palette directly reflects the transition between two sagas: Olympian fury and the frigid desolation of the Nine Realms.

### Primary Palette
- **Spartan Blood-Oath Red (`#8B1E1E`):** Represents war, rage, and Greek heritage. Applied to high-priority interactions, severe warnings, Spartan seals, and ceremonial badges.
- **Gilded Gold Leaf (`#C99738`):** Divine authority and ancient illumination. Serves as secondary emphasis, decorative filigree, active states, and focal iconography.
- **Midgard Frost Cyan (`#388E9C`):** Runic enchantments, leviathan axe chill, and Norse lore anchors. Used for mystical highlights, runic glow, metadata tags, and frostbite status markers.
- **Tome Leather Neutral (`#1A1412`):** Primary canvas base representing ancient tanned hide and weathered ironwood bindings.

### Surface System
- **Leather Exterior (`#1A1412`):** Outer viewport canvas and primary book binding.
- **Inner Binder Plate (`#2C1E19`):** Layered card frames, sidebars, and carved panel bases.
- **Weathered Vellum Light (`#F5EDD6`):** Illuminated manuscript page interiors, reading panes, and parchment insets.
- **Aged Parchment Mid (`#E8DCBE`):** Secondary parchment surfaces, table header rows, and elevated book tabs.
- **Cold Iron Slate (`#252C30`):** Armor plates, weapon statistics, and runic socket containers.

## Typography

The typography system bridges classical antiquity and northern scriptoria.

- **Headlines & Display (`EB Garamond`):** Set with noble proportions, small-caps styling, and deliberate tracking. All major titles evoke stone inscriptions and gilded incipits.
- **Narrative Body (`Literata`):** Designed explicitly for long-form reading, offering deep contrast, calligraphic ductus, and high legibility across dark and vellum backgrounds.
- **Labels & System Codes (`Space Mono`):** Renders technical classifications, bestiary classifications, and Elder Futhark transliterations (`ᚠᚢᚦᚨᚱᚲ`). Its structured grid-aligned rhythm balances the expressive serifs.

### Manuscript Details
- **Drop Capitals:** Paragraph openers in lore codices use 3-line drop caps rendered in gilded gold leaf (`#C99738`) with embossed red backing.
- **Marginalia:** Scribe commentary and Atreus/Mimir journal notes appear in italicized `marginalia` style, offset into margins with tinted ink colors.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout mimics the diptych spread of an illuminated folio bound in thick leather.

### Grid & Spread Architecture
- **Desktop (1200px+):** Two-page spread container framed by leather outer margins (`margin: 3rem`). Left page functions as lore index, bestiary catalogue, or runic map; right page holds the primary illuminated codex entry. Center gutter (`2.5rem`) incorporates a subtle stitched spine crease with a bookmark ribbon overlay.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** Single-page illuminated format with collapsible contextual side-scrolls. Gutters drop to `1.5rem`.
- **Mobile (<768px):** Linear manuscript scroll. Margins tighten to `1rem`. Dual-column content reflows into single stacked scroll blocks. Filigree headers compress into compact Greek key bands.

### Marginalia Placement
Desktop screens provide dedicated 180px asymmetrical outer margins specifically for scholar glosses, runic translations, and warning glyphs.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is tactile and physical rather than modern ambient blur. Elevation reflects layered parchment sheets, embossed tooling, and cast metal appliqués.

### Tiers of Depth
- **Level 0 (Binding Leather `#1A1412`):** Lowest visual plane. Flat textured leather appearance with deep inner shadow (`inset 0 4px 16px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`).
- **Level 1 (Parchment Folio `#F5EDD6` / `#E8DCBE`):** Raised parchment leaf resting inside the binding. Outlined with a dual-tone border (1px solid `#A89874` outer, 1px solid `#C99738` inner). Casts a hard 4px drop shadow (`4px 6px 14px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`) onto the leather base.
- **Level 2 (Inlaid Iron & Bronze Plates `#252C30` / `#2C1E19`):** Sub-cards, statistics plaques, and relic inventories. Inset 2px into the parchment with etched chiseling (`box-shadow: inset 1px 1px 3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6), 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`).
- **Level 3 (Relic Seals & Floating Clasps):** High-level overlays, modal seals, and floating bookmark ribbons. Elevated with sharp, dense shading (`0 10px 24px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75), 0 2px 4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`).
- **Runic Luminescence:** Interactive magical objects bypass physical elevation using an ethereal cyan aura (`0 0 12px rgba(56, 142, 156, 0.55)`).

## Shapes

The codex rejects modern rounded radii entirely in favor of historic geometric precision, beveled stone cuts, and hand-sheared deckle edges.

- **Sharp Edges (`roundedness: 0`):** All containers, parchment pages, panels, buttons, and status tags maintain strict 0px corners.
- **Chamfered & Beveled Clippings:** Specialized relic sockets and primary action triggers utilize 45-degree corner notches (`polygon clip-path`) echoing Spartan shield plates and carved Norse runestones.
- **Deckle Edges:** Parchment containers employ irregular or scalloped page edges on outer margins to simulate hand-torn vellum leaves.
- **Filigree Friezes:** Section headers are framed by continuous Greek meander key lines (`SVG pattern`) or woven Norse braided serpent knots terminating in sharp triangular arrows.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Clasps
- **Primary (Spartan Blood Oath):** Deep crimson (`#8B1E1E`) background, 1px gilded brass border (`#C99738`), sharp chamfered corners. Label in small-caps `EB Garamond`. On hover: border glows bright gold with a subtle inner bronze wash.
- **Secondary (Cold Iron):** Dark iron texture (`#252C30`), cold cyan outline (`#388E9C`), rune prefix. On hover: runes illuminate with `#388E9C` glow.
- **Class / Page Clasps:** Square, bronze hinged-style toggles that audibly and visually depress when engaged.

### Chips & Runic Badges
- **Faction Tags:** Rectangular, sharp badges with a dark stone background and an inner border colored by pantheon (Crimson for Olympus, Cyan for Midgard, Gilded Gold for Asgard). Accompanied by authentic Elder Futhark prefix glyphs.

### Cards & Codex Entries
- **Grimoire Cards:** Parchment texture (`#F5EDD6`), dark ink text (`#1A1412`), framed by meander corners. Headers separated by gold-leaf horizontal dividing rules with an embossed central Greek omega or Norse valknut icon.
- **Relic / Weapon Cards:** Forged cold iron slabs with inlaid stats, runic sockets, and parchment lore tabs tucked behind them.

### Input Fields & Search Inscriptions
- **Search Scribe Field:** Deep parchment-tinted depression (`#E8DCBE` in light reading mode, `#14100F` in tome mode) with engraved inner shadow. Placeholder text appears in muted charcoal calligraphic serif. Blinking cursor styled as a gilded stylus.

### Checkboxes & Selection Marks
- **Checkboxes:** Square iron-framed boxes. Checked state is marked with a blood-red Greek Lambda (`Λ`) or carved Nordic Algiz (`ᛉ`) rune instead of a generic checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons:** Square rotated 45 degrees (diamond lozenge) with a central glowing gold pip upon selection.

### Lists & Bestiary Catalogues
- Ordered lists use classical Roman numerals (`I.`, `II.`, `III.`) or Norse rune tallies. Dividers between catalogue items consist of fine hairline borders centered with small bronze diamond ornaments.

### Specialty Components
- **Bookmark Ribbons:** Hanging velvet ribbons (`#8B1E1E` or `#388E9C`) anchoring from the codex head down the page, acting as quick navigation or reading-progress anchors.
- **Illuminated Drop Caps:** Standalone manuscript capitals wrapping narrative text, with gold foil background and blood-red line work.
- **Marginalia Sticky Notes:** Scribe-written calligraphic callouts pinned by iron rivets into the outer page boundaries.
## Implementación en este proyecto

Este documento es el sistema que generó Stitch (`docs/stitch-referencia.png` es su pantalla de ficha de personaje).
El código lo sigue, con estas adaptaciones:

- **Tokens:** `src/index.css` (`@theme` de Tailwind v4) toma la paleta de superficies (cuero `#1A1412`, placa `#2C1E19`,
  hierro `#252C30`, vitela `#F5EDD6`) y los tres acentos (sangre `#8B1E1E`, oro `#C99738`, escarcha `#388E9C`), más
  variantes claras y oscuras para cumplir contraste 4.5:1 sobre cuero y sobre vitela.
- **Tipografía:** EB Garamond en versalitas (`small-caps`) para títulos y etiquetas, Literata para el texto y Space Mono
  solo para números de folio. Las runas usan Noto Sans Runic y el griego EB Garamond, porque Space Mono no trae esos
  alfabetos.
- **Sin etiquetas encima de los títulos ni textos unidos con "·":** el mock de Stitch los usaba en todas partes.
- **Contenido:** el mock inventaba armas, estadísticas y lore; aquí todo sale de la API (`/api/characters` y
  `/api/mythologies`).
- **Díptico:** inicio (folio griego y nórdico) y detalle (retrato y ficha a la izquierda, página de vitela a la derecha),
  cosidos por un lomo con cinta. En pantallas chicas las páginas se apilan.
- **Cinta-marcador:** el control de favoritos de las tarjetas.
- **Movimiento:** un solo momento orquestado (el códice se abre desde el lomo en el inicio, solo en escritorio) y
  respuestas a la interacción; todo se detiene con `prefers-reduced-motion`.
